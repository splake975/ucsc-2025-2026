Before starting my master’s at UC Santa Cruz, I took time away from school after UC Davis to work as an advantaged player in an online gaming market. I knew from the start that it would not last long, and it ended when California changed its regulations. For most of that time I worked on risk management, deciding how much exposure to take on and how to limit losses. The work gave me practical experience with probability and with collaborating with strangers: most of the people I worked with were anonymous, spread across the country, and from many different backgrounds. Later I moved into software and hardware automation. When the market closed, I returned to my studies.
