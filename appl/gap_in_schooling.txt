Between my time at UC Davis and my current studies at UC Santa Cruz, I took time away from school to work in an online gambling market. I knew from the start that it would not last long, and it ended when California changed its regulations. For most of that time I worked on risk management, deciding how much exposure to take on and how to limit losses. Later I moved into software and hardware automation. The work gave me practical experience with probability and with collaborating with strangers: most of the people I worked with were anonymous, spread across the country, and from many different backgrounds. When the market closed, I returned to my studies, and I am now a master’s student at UC Santa Cruz.
