Before starting my master’s at UC Santa Cruz, I took time away from school to work as an advantaged player on online betting platforms, independently finding positive expected value opportunities in unconventional markets rather than in traditional games like poker,  blackjack or sports betting. I knew it would not last long and it ended when California changed its regulations. I spent most of that time on risk management, deciding how much exposure to take on, and later moved into software and hardware automation. The work gave me practical experience with probability and with collaborating with anonymous strangers across the country from many backgrounds. When the market closed, I returned to my studies.
