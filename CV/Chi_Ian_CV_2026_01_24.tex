    \item \textbf{Labratory work developing new liquid scintillator mixtures for ANNIE and DUNE, Neutrino Experiments}\\
    Working with Dr. Robert Svoboda, I have been in the lab working on new liquid scintillator materials that are projected to be used in the DUNE experiment. These materials are promised to be able to distinguish the light signals caused by Chernekov radiation and scintillation by delaying the emission of scintillation light. These new materials are called Water Based Slow Scintillators (WBSS). My work involved testing how much and what new materials work in delaying the release of scintillation light. I also worked on creating a double coincidence light yield setup to test the scintillators. 
    \item \textbf{Participant in the Research on Rails (Research Trip)}\\
    Lead by Dr. Andrew Waldron, worked directly with PhD candidate Petr Vlachopulos. The paper I read on this trip was \emph{Topological quantum field theory} by Atiyah M. F. The paper is an introduction to TQFT and discusses goes over useful categories in regards to QFT. The category of manifolds of same dimension with morphisms being cobordisms and the category of vector spaces with arrows being linear maps are shown to be related with an interesting functor. the specifics of such a functor is useful in studying QFTs on non-flat spacetimes and the paper goes over several introductory examples of such interesting cases.
